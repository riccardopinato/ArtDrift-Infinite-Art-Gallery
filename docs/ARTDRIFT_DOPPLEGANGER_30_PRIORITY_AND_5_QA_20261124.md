Test 5 exact identities, 300 daily selections, re-open same day, rollback clock; separate 320 dp rendering fixture. Android/Web compile, actual photo GET image check and physical visual review still independent.
- **Rights**: CC license on *photograph* may permit copyright reuse with credit; it is not automatically a permission for endorsements, publicity or commercial association in all jurisdictions. Images and celebrity identities need item/market/use-specific review before production. Source of painting, museum/photographer and original version must be checked. `commercialPublicityCleared=false` in each pair and `RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_RUNTIME` for 37 leads.

- **Schiele** *Self-Portrait with Striped Shirt* is a drawing; V2 marks `DRAWING_CONFIRMED_EXCLUDE_FROM_PAINTING_ONLY`; the user may later choose an “artworks” (rather than painting) scope.
